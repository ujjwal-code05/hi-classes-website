🏫 About Us

Building Extraordinary
Students Since 2019

Him Classes is a premier coaching institute based in Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh, dedicated to nurturing academic excellence across all major educational boards. We believe every student possesses extraordinary potential — our mission is to unlock it.

Our institute offers structured coaching for students from Class 3 to Class 12 across CBSE, ICSE, ISC, and UP Board curricula, with a focus on conceptual clarity, regular practice, and personalised mentorship.
